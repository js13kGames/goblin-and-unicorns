Play as either a 🧌Goblin or a pack of 🦄Unicorns to achieve their unique objectives.

The goblin can jump 🦄Unicorns, removing them from the board. The 🧌Goblin wins when there are fewer than four 🦄Unicorns left on the board. Multiple jumps in one turn are allowed.

🦄Unicorns must trap the 🧌Goblin. 🦄Unicorns win when the 🧌Goblin has no valid moves on their turn.
